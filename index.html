04 / BEFORE YOU START

A LITTLE CLARITY.
A LOT OF POTENTIAL.

Your first step doesn't have to be complicated.

I'm new to training. Where do I start?+

Start with Strength Foundations or Pilates & Mobility. Focus on learning the movements at a comfortable pace. The starting-plan builder below can help you choose a direction.

How do I choose the right program?+

Pick the goal that matters most to you: building strength, improving conditioning, or moving more freely. Open any program card to see its focus, format, and suggested experience level.

Do I need equipment?+

It depends on the program. Strength and kettlebell sessions use gym equipment; mobility and cardio can start with a mat and space to move. Each program card includes equipment guidance.

Does the starting plan book a session?+

No. It creates a simple training outline in your browser. You can review and download it, but it does not reserve a class, send a request, or share your information.
